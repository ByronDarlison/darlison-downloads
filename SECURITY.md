# Security and privacy

The files in this repository are text prompts and templates. They do not require credentials and should not contain executable secrets.

## Protect your information

- Review the privacy and data-retention terms of the artificial intelligence service you use.
- Remove confidential information when it is not required for the exercise.
- Do not commit completed assessments, business plans, financial records, employee information, client information, transcripts, or credentials to this repository.
- Treat generated output as a draft that requires human review.

## Reporting a concern

Do not disclose a security or privacy concern in a public issue if the report contains sensitive information. Contact Byron Darlison through [darlison.com](https://www.darlison.com/) with a concise description of the affected file and the risk.
