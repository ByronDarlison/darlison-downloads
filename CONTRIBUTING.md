# Contributing

Corrections and practical improvements are welcome.

## Before opening a change

- Keep the language precise, direct, and simple.
- Preserve the purpose and required output of the prompt.
- Do not add private company information, client information, credentials, transcripts, or generated outputs.
- Verify every attribution you add.
- Explain why the change improves the process for someone using it without coaching support.

## Proposing a change

1. Open an issue describing the problem.
2. Fork the repository and create a focused branch.
3. Change only the files needed to solve that problem.
4. Review the complete prompt after editing it. A local correction can affect instructions later in the conversation.
5. Open a pull request explaining what changed, why it changed, and how you checked it.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's existing license.
